Screedio Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026 · Türkçe: Gizlilik Politikası

Screedio ("the app") is made by Yasin KENDİR ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: we do not collect your data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking tools. We do not receive, store, or have access to your screenshots, photos, boards, or any other content you create.

What stays on your device

  • Screenshots and images you choose, the cutouts and boards you create, and text recognized from images. All of this is processed on your device using Apple’s Vision framework and is stored in the app’s storage on your device.
  • App settings and your free-tier usage counts.
  • The free-tier usage counts are kept in a small local record on your device (your device’s Keychain), so reinstalling the app does not reset them. This record never leaves your device.

Deleting the app removes the content stored by the app on your device. If you have device backups (such as iCloud Backup) turned on, the app’s data may be included in them. That is controlled by your device settings and Apple, not by us.

Photo library access

The app asks for access to your photos only so you can pick images to work with. You can limit access to selected photos or deny it in Settings. Images are never uploaded to us.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not see your payment details or Apple ID. Apple’s privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your information to anyone. If you export or share an image from the app, you do so through your device’s share sheet and choose the destination yourself.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Data retention and deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. You can delete your content inside the app or by removing the app from your device. You can revoke photo access at any time in iOS Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Material changes will be described in the app’s release notes.
