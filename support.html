Restore a purchase

If you bought the Lifetime unlock before, open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Free tier and Lifetime

Screedio is free to use, with generous limits on some tools. The one-time Lifetime unlock removes those limits. Your existing content always stays available to open and export.

Photo access

Screedio only needs photo access to let you pick images. You can change it any time in Settings, then Screedio, then Photos.

Your data

Everything stays on your device. We don’t collect your content, so there is nothing to delete on our side. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, use reportaproblem.apple.com.
